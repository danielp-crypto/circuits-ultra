Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?

Our showcase websites start at R2,000. Business websites are R4,000, websites with booking functionality are R5,000, and e-commerce stores start from R7,500. Custom development projects are quoted based on requirements.

How long does a website take?

Most showcase websites are completed within 1-2 weeks. Business websites and booking sites typically take 2-3 weeks. E-commerce projects and custom development vary based on complexity.

Do I need my own domain?

Yes, you'll need to purchase your own domain (e.g., yourbusiness.co.za). We can guide you through this process if you need assistance.

Do I need my own hosting?

Yes, you'll need to purchase your own hosting. We help you set up and deploy your website to your hosting. This keeps your costs transparent and gives you full control.

Can you help me set up hosting?

Absolutely. We guide you through selecting a suitable hosting provider and help you configure everything for your website.

Can you build an online store?

Yes, we build e-commerce websites with product catalogues, shopping carts, payment integration, and order management. Pricing starts from R7,500 depending on complexity.
